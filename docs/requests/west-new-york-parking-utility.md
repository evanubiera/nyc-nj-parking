# Request to West New York: showing its municipal lots (SRC-10)

Draft, 2026-11-06. Not sent, and optional: the lots went live without it on 2026-11-06 (ADR-0014). It's still useful for the time limits and the Dewey Triangle lot.

## How to send it

The Parking Utility's [Facilities page](https://www.westnewyorknj.org/pages/parking-services/facilities/) gives its office, at 224 60th Street, and its phone, (201) 295-1575, but no email address. Call and ask which email address to send this to, or post it to the office. If they answer by phone, note who said what and when, for SRC-10.

Before sending: add your name. The draft doesn't say whether the app is free or commercial (PRD-6).

## Message

> Hello,
>
> I'm building NYC and NJ public parking, a web app that shows people public parking near them in New York City and New Jersey. It lists only parking run by public agencies, and shows where each listing comes from, with a link back to the source.
>
> I'd like to include West New York's municipal parking lots, using your Facilities page: each lot's name, location, and number of spots, the meter hours (Monday to Saturday, 9:00am to 9:00pm), the meter rate (25¢ per 20 minutes), and the overnight pass ($10.00, 9:01pm to 9:00am). Each lot would be credited to the West New York Parking Utility, with a link to your page.
>
> Could you help with these?
>
> 1. May we show this information in the app, credited and linked as above? Is there any wording you'd like us to use?
> 2. Your page says the maximum allotment varies by lot. Could you tell us each lot's time limit?
> 3. Where exactly is the 6701 Dewey Triangle Lot? We couldn't place it on a map.
> 4. Is there a better place to check for changes, such as new lots or rates, than the Facilities page?
>
> We leave out the reserved garages, since they're monthly only.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name]
