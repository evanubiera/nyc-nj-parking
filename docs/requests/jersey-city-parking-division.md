# Request to Jersey City: its public parking lots (SRC-5)

Draft, 2026-11-04. Not sent. To go with [jersey-city-parking-candidates.csv](jersey-city-parking-candidates.csv).

## How to send it

1. **Open data portal form**, [data.jerseycitynj.gov/pages/contact-us](https://data.jerseycitynj.gov/pages/contact-us/). It goes to the city's data team, who can pass it to the Parking Division. It takes a name, an email, and a message (no attachments), behind a CAPTCHA, so you submit it yourself. The message offers to send the spreadsheet once someone replies.
2. **Or phone the Parking Division**, (201) 547-5538 (Two Jackson Square, 356 Martin Luther King Dr.), and ask who to send the list to. Its page lists email addresses only for permits and signs.
3. **If there's no answer after a few weeks**, a records request through [OPRA Online – Jersey City](https://jerseycity.mycusthelp.com/WEBAPP/_rs/(S(5bnhahjdyem5gx3awytcgkrn))/SupportHome.aspx) (the City Clerk takes OPRA requests only there). Suggested wording is at the end. OPRA gives existing records; it doesn't settle whether we may republish them, so question 4 still needs an answer from the city.

Before sending: add your name, and say whether the app is free or commercial only once PRD-6 is decided. The draft doesn't claim either.

## Message

> Hello,
>
> I'm building NYC and NJ public parking, a web app that shows people public parking near them in New York City and New Jersey. It lists only parking run by public agencies, and shows where each listing comes from, with a link back to the source.
>
> I'd like to include Jersey City's municipal lots. The open data portal's Municipal Parking Lots dataset lists 7 lots and was last updated in November 2020, so I'd like to make sure what we show is current and complete.
>
> Could the Parking Division help with these?
>
> 1. A current list of the city's public parking lots and garages: address, number of spaces, hours, rates, and whether each is open to everyone or to permit holders only.
> 2. A check of 73 sites on public land that the State's parcel and tax records describe as parking (NJOGIS, August 2026). They include the 7 lots above; some are probably staff or school lots. I have them in a spreadsheet with a column for each question above, and can send it to whoever can look at it.
> 3. Whether this information is kept up to date anywhere, or whether the Municipal Parking Lots dataset on the portal could be updated.
> 4. Whether we may show this information in the app, credited to the City of Jersey City with a link to the city's page, and any credit wording you'd like us to use.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name]

## If you phone

> Hi, I'm building a public parking finder for New York City and New Jersey, and I'd like to include Jersey City's municipal lots. I have a list of sites to confirm and some questions about hours and rates. Who in the Parking Division should I send it to, and at what email address?

## OPRA wording, if needed

> Any current list, schedule, or inventory of off-street parking lots and garages owned or operated by the City of Jersey City, including for each its address, number of spaces, hours, rates, and permit requirements.
