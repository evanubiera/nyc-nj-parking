# Request to Weehawken: its municipal lots (SRC-11)

Draft, 2026-11-07. Not sent. Weehawken publishes no list of its lots: the Township's website has no page about parking, and its code on eCode360 can't be republished (specs/06). An answer would let the app show Weehawken's lots as confirmed, run by the Parking Authority, instead of one unconfirmed site from tax records.

## How to send it

The Township's website gives no contact for the Parking Authority of the Township of Weehawken. Call the Township at (201) 319-6005, or write to it at 400 Park Avenue, Weehawken, NJ 07086, and ask where to send this. If they answer by phone, note who said what and when, for SRC-11.

Before sending: add your name. The draft doesn't say whether the app is free or commercial (PRD-6).

## Message

> Hello,
>
> I'm building NYC and NJ public parking, a web app that shows people public parking near them in New York City and New Jersey. It lists only parking run by public agencies, and shows where each listing comes from, with a link back to the source.
>
> I'd like to include the Weehawken Parking Authority's municipal parking lots. The Township's code (Chapter 8) describes several as parking meter zones: under the Lincoln Tunnel approach along JFK Boulevard East, by the Marginal Highways; at the end of Gracie Place; part of the lot at Gregory Avenue and Hackensack Plank Road; and the upper lot beside Town Hall.
>
> Could you help with these?
>
> 1. Which lots does the Parking Authority run today, and which are open to the public rather than to permit holders only?
> 2. For each lot: its address or location, its number of spaces, and its hours, rates, and time limits. The code sets $1 an hour from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. and $2 an hour overnight, "unless otherwise posted". Is that still right?
> 3. Is there a page, map, or document listing the lots that we could link to and check for changes?
> 4. May we show this information in the app, credited to the Parking Authority? Is there any wording you'd like us to use?
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name]
